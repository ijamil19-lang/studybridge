StudyBridge V2 — Booking-style navigation and search

Upload ALL of these files into the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repository:
index.html, search.html, universities.html, opportunities.html, styles.css, app.js, privacy.html

Do not upload the enclosing folder; all files must sit beside index.html.
The home search submits to search.html with filters in the URL.
Results use Supabase public read-only endpoints. Countries are selectable even when no matching records exist.
The privacy policy is a draft; add your contact information and review before publication.
Test programme filters, universities, and opportunity tables against your live database.
Never place Supabase service-role keys in public files.
