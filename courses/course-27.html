“1 Decembrie 1918” University of Alba Iulia

About this programme

Accredited undergraduate programme offered by the Faculty of History, Letters and Educational Sciences at “1 Decembrie 1918” University of Alba Iulia. The programme prepares specialists in archaeology and leads to qualifications including archaeologist, historian and local promoter.
