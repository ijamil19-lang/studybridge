Registered Nurse, Bachelor of Health Care

Savonia University of Applied Sciences

About this programme

Savonia English-taught 210 ECTS Bachelor of Health Care (Registered Nurse) at Microkatu campus in Kuopio. Clinical training and Finnish-language learning are built into the programme; nursing registration in Finland is separately licensed. University's first academic year fee is €9,700 before scholarships.
