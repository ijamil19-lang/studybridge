Babeș-Bolyai University

About this programme

Babeș-Bolyai University's Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science lists a full-time Information Engineering Bachelor degree offered in English in Cluj-Napoca. The official faculty profile states an eight-semester curriculum, 240 ECTS credits and coverage of computing hardware, software and communications components, data representation, scientific problem-solving and mathematics. The university's 2026–2027 list identifies the English-taught programme with provisional functioning authorization.
