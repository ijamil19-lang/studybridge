Veterinary Medicine (English-taught integrated degree)

“Ion Ionescu de la Brad” Iași University of Life Sciences

Programme overview

Ion Ionescu de la Brad Iași University of Life Sciences describes a six-year Veterinary Medicine programme taught in English. The university's dedicated Veterinary Medicine international admission requirements state that Romanian, EU and non-EU citizens may apply subject to submitting the required documents, an admissions interview and proof of English at minimum B1. The other three faculties primarily teach in Romanian, so their degree programmes should not be mistaken for English-taught pathways. The university admissions page has older update information; confirm whether an upcoming intake, tuition and veterinary professional-recognition conditions have changed.

Source checked 18 October 2026. A programme being listed does not mean applications are open.
