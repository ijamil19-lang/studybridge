About this programme

Babeș-Bolyai University offers an English-taught Bachelor programme in Artificial Intelligence through the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science in Cluj-Napoca. The faculty's official programme profile identifies a six-semester, 180-ECTS, full-time curriculum and published entrance criteria based on Mathematics or Computer Science. The 2026–2027 university list identifies this programme with provisional functioning authorization. Applicants must confirm the current entry requirements, available admission cycle and whether their international applicant category is eligible.
