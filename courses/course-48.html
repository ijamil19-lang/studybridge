European Administration

The National University of Political Studies and Public Administration

About this programme

Undergraduate programme listed by SNSPA under the Faculty of Public Administration. The published subjects include European Administrative Law, European Economy, European Governance, EU decision-making, European Public Policies, regional development, EU financial audit and control, European identity, and management of European projects. (snspa.ro)
