PhD in Global Education

Coventry University

About this programme

Coventry University’s Centre for Global Learning describes this as a structured PhD designed to develop educational researchers with a global outlook. It is aimed at professionals working in educational settings and includes research training, online taught modules, portfolio submissions and an original research project. (coventry.ac.uk)

This is a catalogue snapshot. A published course profile does not establish that applications are open now; always verify current requirements with the university.
