About this programme

Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași publishes a dedicated English-language Civil Engineering Bachelor programme profile through its Faculty of Civil Engineering and Building Services. The official degree description identifies four years and 240 ECTS of full-time study, with engineering science, structural systems, construction materials, geotechnics, building design and supervised practical site visits.
