Bachelor’s Programme in Performing Arts

Stockholm University of the Arts

About this programme

Bachelor-level programme with specialisations including costume design, light design, make-up and wig design, playwriting and dramaturgy, production management, scenography, sound design, technical coordination of production and theatre directing.
