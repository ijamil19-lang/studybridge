Program de formare psihopedagogică în regim postuniversitar

“1 Decembrie 1918” University of Alba Iulia

About this programme

The Department for Teacher Training (DPPD) of “1 Decembrie 1918” University of Alba Iulia organizes a fee-based postgraduate psycho-pedagogical training programme for graduates of UAB and other state or private higher-education institutions. The programme provides Level I and Level II certification for teaching competencies.
