Official Bilingual Master's in International Relations and Migrations

Universidad de Cádiz

About this programme

The University of Cádiz Faculty of Law's 2026–2027 degree page confirms an official Master's in International Relations and Migrations (International and European Studies). The degree examines migration, human rights, European institutions, international relations and policy, with published 2026/27 timetables and a bilingual delivery model. The university's postgraduate data specify a 60-ECTS programme delivered approximately 77% in English and 23% in Spanish, so it should not be described as fully English-taught. The admissions and language requirements for a specific non-Spanish applicant, available places and current application window need to be checked directly.

Official source reviewed 18 October 2026. A course listing is not confirmation of an open application period.
