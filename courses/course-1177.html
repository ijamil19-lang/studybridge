Business Administration and Management with University Expert in International Business

Universidad Europea del Atlántico

About this programme

On-site University of the Atlantic official business administration bachelor with a university expert qualification in international business. Most of the degree is taught in Spanish with some English modules; UNEATLANTICO requires Spanish B2 from non-native speakers in most bachelor programmes. Foreign secondary qualifications require UNED access credential or Spanish degree equivalency as applicable. The expert award is not a second bachelor's degree.
