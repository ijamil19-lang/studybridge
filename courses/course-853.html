Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)

Coventry University

About this programme

Coventry University offers a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), FHEQ Level 8, with original research undertaken under academic supervision through university research centres. The current official research-degree page distinguishes expected from maximum registration periods. A 2024-25 PhD specification describes a four-year full-time or seven-year part-time basis; this is retained as historical supporting documentation rather than substituted for the expected duration. Applicants should confirm centre-specific research opportunities, their study period, fees, eligibility and availability. Official research degrees: https://www.coventry.ac.uk/research/research-opportunities/research-students/research-degrees/?Page=1 ; 2024-25 PhD specification: https://www.coventry.ac.uk/contentassets/d0f3465b0bab40bf86bc7cbba03a4012/phd-specification-part-a-2024-25.pdf ; Official research-degree fees: https://www.coventry.ac.uk/research/research-opportunities/research-students/fees-and-funding/
