Telecommunication Technologies and Systems (English-taught)

Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași

About this programme

Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași offers a Bachelor programme in Telecommunication Technologies and Systems through its Faculty of Electronics, Telecommunications and Information Technology. The official English-language programme profile specifies eight semesters and 240 ECTS. It covers network design, wireless and mobile communications, optical infrastructure, internet of things, signal processing, security and professional internships.
