Individual Project in Moving Image

Royal Institute of Art

About this programme

An 18-credit first-cycle summer course for students with prior knowledge in fine art, film or moving image. The course centres on an independently developed moving-image project supported by individual supervision, seminars and feedback sessions. (kkh.se)
