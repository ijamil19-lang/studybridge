BSc (Hons) Midwifery (Apprenticeship)

Buckinghamshire New University

About this programme

A learner-centred midwifery degree apprenticeship co-produced with employer partners, service users and the University. The programme prepares learners for registered midwifery practice and NMC registration, subject to successful completion of the programme requirements.
