Computer Science BSc

University of Szeged

About this programme

The University of Szeged's Computer Science BSc is a six-semester (180 ECTS) programme for students preparing to develop and manage IT systems. Published requirements include completed secondary education, English at approximately B2 level, and an oral entrance examination covering communication, mathematics and computer science. The listed 2026/27 autumn application period ended in May 2026. Check for a newly announced cycle before applying.
