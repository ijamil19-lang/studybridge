Structural Engineering (English-taught)

Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași

About this programme

Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași's English-taught Structural Engineering Master programme is described individually in the Romanian government's official Study in Romania programme finder. This two-year, 120-ECTS, full-time degree includes numerical and finite-element modelling of civil structures, structural behaviour under earthquake, wind, fire and blast conditions, seismic risk assessment, soil-structure interaction and structural health monitoring.
