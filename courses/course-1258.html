Erasmus Mundus Master's in Urban Climate and Sustainability (MUrCS)

Universidad de Huelva

About this programme

The University of Huelva's official degree programme page describes MUrCS, the Erasmus Mundus Master in Urban Climate and Sustainability, as an on-campus English-taught joint Master's programme. This interdisciplinary degree addresses climate-adapted urban development, planning, environmental management and sustainable cities, bringing together higher education partners in more than one European country. The university's September 2026 governing body approved a modification to the official joint Master's verification documentation, showing the programme remains under institutional consideration. The university description references February applications via the Master's website, but this does not confirm current vacancies or eligibility; check consortium admissions, mobility locations, fees and any scholarship rules.

Official source reviewed 18 October 2026. A course listing is not confirmation of an open application period.
