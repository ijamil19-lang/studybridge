Official Master's in Advanced English Studies and its Applications

Universidad de Vigo

About this programme

The University of Vigo's official Faculty of Philology and Translation study guide for 2026–2027 lists the University Master's Degree in Advanced English Studies and its Applications, an actual postgraduate degree rather than an individual short course. The programme guide includes modules in English for Specific Purposes, applied linguistic research, English-language literature and culture, and language teaching. Individual current modules are explicitly taught in English, but the overall degree teaching-language requirements and any multilingual elements should be confirmed with the university. This faculty-wide curriculum source verifies programme existence, but not individual international applicant eligibility, fees or an open application period.

Official source reviewed 18 October 2026. A course listing is not confirmation of an open application period.
