Medicine (English-taught integrated degree)

“Dunărea de Jos” University of Galați

Programme overview

Dunărea de Jos University of Galați's official international admissions language-of-instruction page identifies Medicine as a full professional study programme taught in English by its Faculty of Medicine and Pharmacy. Clinical internships, seminars and practical work are conducted in Romanian beginning in the fourth year because students need to communicate with patients. Its international study offer describes a six-year medical degree, not an ordinary three-year Bachelor. Individual international eligibility, recognition of qualifications and future intakes must be confirmed with the university. The cited 2025 and 2026 admissions information does not prove applications are currently open.

Source checked 18 October 2026. A programme being listed does not mean applications are open.
