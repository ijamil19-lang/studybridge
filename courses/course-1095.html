Bachelor's Degree in Applied Mathematics

IQS- Universitat Ramon Llull

About this programme

Joint IQS and La Salle Ramon Llull official bachelor's degree. Teaching is in Spanish in year one, Spanish/Catalan in year two, then English in years three and four; it must not be advertised as English-only. International admissions documentation is provided.
