BA (Hons) Business English (International Route) Level 6 Top-Up

University of Chichester

About this programme

A one-academic-year, full-time, in-person Level 6 top-up course for international students. The programme combines business studies with academic English, business English, international business communication and research skills, and awards a full BA (Hons) degree on successful completion. (chi.ac.uk)
