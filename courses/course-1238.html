About this programme

Constanța Maritime University's official 2026 admission and non-EU candidate pages identify a four-year, 240-ECTS English-taught Bachelor degree in Nautical Science / Navigation and Waterborne Transport. The course prepares students for maritime navigation and onboard operational responsibilities. The university mentions shipboard practice and national certificate-of-competency conditions, which should not be assumed equivalent across countries.
