About this programme

The University of Medicine and Pharmacy of Craiova's official international-citizen 2026–2027 admission page offers Medicine in its English teaching section as a six-year professional qualification carrying 360 ECTS. The university separately publishes EU and non-EU seat categories and an €8,500 annual tuition figure for that admissions cycle. It states that clinical practical work is conducted in Romanian from year four. Its file-based application period ended on 15 July 2026; recheck language, tuition, qualifications and next intake.

Source review: 18 October 2026. Admission deadlines and published place numbers may relate to the 2026–2027 cycle and are not live application availability.
