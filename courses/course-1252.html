Automation and Applied Informatics (English-taught)

Aurel Vlaicu University of Arad

Programme overview

Aurel Vlaicu University of Arad announced an additional English-taught Bachelor programme in Automation and Applied Informatics for autumn 2026. Its official university news identifies a full-time 240-ECTS engineering degree approved under Romanian Government Decision 606/2026, with 30 places in the announcement. The faculty's study-plan catalogue confirms the four-year Automation and Applied Informatics academic subject. The university's non-EU candidate page says international applicants can apply to available programmes under published rules, but its 2026 submission period closed on 14 September. Verify the next intake, English group and individual eligibility directly with the university.

Source checked 18 October 2026. A programme being listed does not mean applications are open.
