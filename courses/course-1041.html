Bachelor's Degree Programme in Immersive Software Engineering and AI

University of Jyväskylä

About this programme

English-taught immersive software engineering and AI degree (180 ECTS), includes two internships and 2027 selection process with online academic readiness screening and interview. Internships are not guaranteed paid.
