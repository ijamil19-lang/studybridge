Early Childhood Development and Learning BA (Hons)

Coventry University

About this programme

An undergraduate course focused on childhood development and learning, including early-years practice, child development, play, safeguarding, inclusion, pedagogy, research, and leadership. The course states that successful completion leads to a BA (Hons) degree in Early Childhood Development and Learning.
