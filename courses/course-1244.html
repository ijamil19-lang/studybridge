National Academy of Music “Gheorghe Dima”

Programme overview

Gheorghe Dima National Academy of Music officially identifies a Music Bachelor degree taught in English as part of its 2026 international study offer. The programme lasts three years, awards 180 ECTS and is listed as full-time. It is separate from the four-year Musical Performance programmes for singing and instruments. The academy's English programmes announcement contains the programme-level overview, but not the precise audition, applicant-specific eligibility or future application schedule. Verify the 2027 or later offer, documents, fees and admissions directly with the university.

Source checked 18 October 2026. A programme being listed does not mean applications are open.
