Cyber Security (English-taught)

Transilvania University of Brașov

About this programme

Transilvania University of Brașov publishes an English-taught Cyber Security Master's programme within electronics, telecommunications and information technologies. Its official curriculum overview covers information security, cryptography, ethical hacking, identity and access management, critical infrastructure protection and incident response. The university's June 2026 admissions announcement describes a dual-degree collaboration with a Swiss institution. The university advises international candidates to ask its international office whether an individual study programme is available to their applicant category. Its published 2026 non-EU application period ended in July 2026; the next admission period has not been confirmed.
