PhD in Sociology

The National University of Political Studies and Public Administration

About this programme

SNSPA officially organizes doctoral studies in Sociology. The specific doctoral-school page describes the Sociology doctoral programme as focused on empirical and theoretical study of Romanian society, with sociological and anthropological approaches. Official SNSPA information also identifies political sociology and post-socialism anthropology among doctoral research topics. (doctorat.snspa.ro)

This page is a snapshot of verified catalogue information, not an offer of admission. Check all current information using the official university link.
