Engineering and Management in Transport and Logistics — English-taught

Constanta Maritime University

About this programme

Constanța Maritime University publishes a dedicated official English-taught Bachelor of Science profile for Engineering and Management in Transport and Logistics. The course lasts four years and combines transport engineering, supply-chain management, logistics operations, economics, sustainable mobility and digitalisation in a maritime-port environment. The university's international admissions conditions require official document review, English language evidence and tuition arrangements. Published 2026 applications have closed; future intake must be checked.

Source review: 18 October 2026. Admission deadlines and published place numbers may relate to the 2026–2027 cycle and are not live application availability.
