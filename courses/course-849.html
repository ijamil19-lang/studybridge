Masters by Research

Coventry University

About this programme

Coventry University describes the Masters by Research as a research programme in which postgraduate researchers undertake research with academic support from a supervisory team and engage in researcher-development activities. The programme may serve as a progression route toward doctoral study. (coventry.ac.uk)

This is a catalogue snapshot. A published course profile does not establish that applications are open now; always verify current requirements with the university.
