Computer Science Engineering BSc

University of Debrecen

About this programme

The University of Debrecen offers an English-taught Computer Science Engineering BSc lasting seven semesters (210 ECTS). The degree combines computing with an engineering approach, including programming, computer architecture, computer networks, embedded systems, databases and IT security. Official entry information includes a high-school certificate, B2-level English and subject entrance assessments. Check the university for the latest intake and application details.
