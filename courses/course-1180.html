Social Pedagogy MA

University of Nyíregyháza

About this programme

English-taught two-year Master's in Social Pedagogy at the University of Nyíregyháza. Applicants usually have prior Social Pedagogy or Social Work BA or equivalent relevant study credits and must complete an online English interview at CEFR B2; a separate language certificate is not always compulsory. Tuition is quoted per academic year.
