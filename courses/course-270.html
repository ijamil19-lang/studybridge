Bachelor’s programme in Jewellery and Corpus: Ädellab

Konstfack, University of Arts, Crafts and Design

About this programme

Develops artistic and technical practice in jewellery and corpus through exploration of materials, techniques, history, contemporary expression, critical thinking, workshops, seminars and exhibitions.
