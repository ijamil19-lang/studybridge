International Master in South European Studies (EUROSUD)

Universidad Autónoma de Madrid

About this programme

English-taught UAM-led international double/multiple Master's delivered in Madrid for year one and a partner university abroad for year two. The Aix-Marseille route also requires French B2. Applications from foreign-degree holders are explicitly permitted, subject to degree equivalence, prior social sciences/humanities and language tests. Non-EU first-year fee for 2026/27 differs from year-two amount.

This page is a snapshot of verified catalogue information, not an offer of admission. Check all current information using the official university link.
