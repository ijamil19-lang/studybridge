Finding a suitable course in Italy

Italian higher education uses different qualification cycles, and courses can be taught in Italian, English or both. A degree title alone does not establish the language, applicant category or admissions route.

Start with your preferred degree and subject, then open the official university programme page. Availability, fees and admission criteria can change. StudyBridge provides research leads; it does not submit applications or guarantee a place, scholarship or visa.

Before you apply: Confirm whether the programme is a Laurea, Laurea Magistrale or doctoral course, and check its teaching language and entry criteria.
