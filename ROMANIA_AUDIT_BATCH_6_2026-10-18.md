### Accuracy caveats
- **Medical/veterinary qualifications:** The six-year English Veterinary Medicine and English Medicine qualifications are *integrated professional degrees*. They are classified as `degree_level=Other`, not ordinary three-year Bachelor's degrees.
- **Galați Medicine:** The university says practical clinical training, seminars and internships are in **Romanian starting from year four** despite an English-language degree route.
- **Bacău:** The English–French philology Bachelor's programme is **bilingual**, not an entirely English-taught engineering or business degree. Other language Master's programme names may evolve; international eligibility for all three Bacău records is `NULL` pending individual verification.
- **Iași Veterinary Medicine:** An official source supports international admission criteria and B1 English, but the university webpage reflects older updates; a future intake is not guaranteed.
- **Arad new degree:** The university's August 2026 official announcement identifies an English-taught full-time 240-ECTS programme; the published 2026 non-EU admissions calendar ended 14 September 2026.
- **Bacău financial warning:** 2026 foreign-admission guidance describes an attendance-guarantee payment for eligible applicants. Do not pay until confirming current written terms, amount, refund conditions and eligibility with the university.
- **Music degrees:** The academy's English degree announcement is an event dated July 2026. That announcement describes what it offered, but **not** an open October admission round.
- **Galați reference date:** The university teaching-language list includes a 2025-era header; confirm all three programmes and their teaching language in the upcoming admissions cycle.
- Every entry is a research listing, not confirmed open admission, scholarship, visa or guaranteed individual eligibility.
