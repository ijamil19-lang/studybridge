Privacy policy

How UniRouteFinder handles searches and AI-assisted course research.

Last reviewed: 17 October 2026

Website use and university searches

UniRouteFinder is an informational website. Search terms and filter selections are processed in your browser to find published university data. We retrieve public catalogue records from Supabase, and hosting providers may handle standard technical logs such as IP addresses, request times and browser details.

The website currently does not provide student accounts, application submission or payments. University websites and other linked services have their own privacy policies.

Ask AI matching

If you use Ask AI, the country, questions, qualifications and any nationality or preferences you voluntarily enter — as well as limited recent conversation context — are sent to OpenAI to produce course suggestions. Do not submit passports, contact details, health information or other sensitive personal data. UniRouteFinder does not save your questions as an ongoing student profile. Short-lived, one-way hashed network identifiers are used to enforce usage limits.

The AI suggestions do not establish personal admission, visa, scholarship or international-student eligibility. Always check current information with the university.

External services and links

UniRouteFinder uses GitHub Pages for the public website, Supabase for course data and AI request processing, and OpenAI for Ask AI. Some pages load third-party images from Unsplash; these services may receive technical connection information when accessed. Official university and funding links lead to independently operated websites.

Contact and questions

A direct privacy contact address has not yet been provided by the site operator. This policy needs an owner contact method and a final legal review before it can be regarded as complete.
