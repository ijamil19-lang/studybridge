Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

StudyBridge is an informational website. Search terms and filter selections are processed in your browser to help you explore university opportunities. The site requests public listing data from Supabase and may load resources from third-party providers such as Google Fonts and Unsplash. Those providers may receive standard technical information, including your IP address, when their resources are requested.

This version does not provide user accounts, application submission or payment functionality. It does not intentionally collect personal information through forms. Hosting and service providers may process technical access logs according to their policies.

StudyBridge links to external university and scholarship websites, which have their own privacy policies. Please review those policies before sharing information with them.

Site owner: Before publication, add your business contact details and review this policy against the services you actually use. This template is not legal advice.

Ask AI course matching

When you use Ask AI, your selected destination, question, qualifications, nationality (if provided), and preferences are sent to an AI service operated by OpenAI so suitable courses can be suggested from StudyBridge's published catalogue. Please avoid contact details, passport numbers or sensitive personal information. StudyBridge does not save the question as a student profile. Hashed network identifiers are used to limit abuse and protect service costs. Suggestions are informational only and do not guarantee admission, visa approval, funding or international eligibility.
