## Quality and measurement rules

1. Put search-intent language naturally into **unique titles, meta descriptions, visible H1/H2 text, and helpful content**, not repetitive lists of terms.
2. Google's Search documentation says the `meta name="keywords"` tag **does not affect indexing or ranking**. We intentionally do not add it.
3. Avoid wording that implies admissions are open, scholarships guaranteed, or international-student eligibility verified unless the underlying record explicitly supports it.
4. All individual programme pages should emphasise their **specific course name and university**. Do not automatically insert the identical generic keyword sentence into hundreds of course profiles.
5. Monitor indexed vs excluded pages, search query impressions, CTR, and content usefulness after Google has crawled the domain. Do not assume keywords have measurable demand until supported by actual data.
6. Confirm the public domain `https://www.uniroutefinder.com/` and indexed canonical URLs continue working; the site's current XML sitemap is `/sitemap.xml`.
