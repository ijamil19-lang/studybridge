Finding a suitable course in Finland

A programme advertised in English may still have specific language requirements for placements or professional practice. Check the published selection criteria, language tests and programme-specific academic prerequisites.

Start with your preferred degree and subject, then open the official university programme page. Availability, fees and admission criteria can change. UniRouteFinder provides research leads; it does not submit applications or guarantee a place, scholarship or visa.
